Head Coach · Varsity Girls Basketball

Dave
McCall

Coach Dave McCall has been shaping athletes and building programs in Santa Rosa for decades. A Division I basketball player himself, he brings the standard of elite competition to every practice, every clinic, and every conversation with his players.

Over more than 30 years coaching, Coach McCall has worked with athletes who have competed at every level — from high school varsity through collegiate and professional play. His philosophy is simple: Carrillo basketball comes first.

"To compete against the best and play at your best, you have to put in the work individually and build chemistry with your teammates."
— Coach Dave McCall, Head Coach · Varsity Girls Basketball

What You Need to Know

Before You
Show Up

👗 Equipment Required

  • Reversible jersey — required for all league and tournament games
  • Basketball shoes with proper court grip
  • Water bottle — every session, every day
  • Athletic clothing appropriate for conditioning work

📋 Program Expectations

  • Carrillo basketball comes before outside club basketball — this is the standard
  • JV and Varsity players compete together all summer
  • Younger players may be invited to select varsity tournaments at Coach's discretion
  • Commitment to the full summer program is the expectation

💵 Fees at a Glance

  • $250 — Standard summer registration (clinic + league)
  • $350 — Registration fee if accepted to Chico State Tournament
  • Payment via Zelle® to (707) 696-3816 at time of registration
  • Chico State team fee ($575/team) is handled separately by Coach

🏥 Health & Safety

  • Health insurance coverage required for Chico State Tournament
  • Physical exam within past year required for Chico camp participation
  • Inform Coach of any medical conditions at registration
  • Emergency contacts must be on file before first session
